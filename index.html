Before Your Appointment

What to have ready

  • Valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID for every signer (driver's license, state ID or passport).
  • Your complete, unsigned documents. Please don't sign ahead of time — you'll sign in front of the notary.
  • All signers present at the appointment.
  • Witnesses, if your document requires them. Wills and powers of attorney in Florida require two witnesses. Please bring your own, or ask when you book; we may be able to arrange witnesses with advance notice.
Credentials

Commissioned, screened & insured

  • Florida Commissioned Notary Public — Commission #HH 753427, valid through January 7, 2030
  • NNA Certified Notary Signing Agent — certified through the National Notary Association
  • Registered Florida Remote Online Notary
  • Background screened — National Notary Association (NNA) background check, June 2026
  • Errors & Omissions insured — $25,000 policy through Western Surety Company
